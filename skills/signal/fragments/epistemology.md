# Epistemology

Per load-bearing claim: `claim | evidence | falsifier | uncertainty owner | leverage | limits`.

Separate observation/hypothesis/decision. Confidence ≠ evidence; source/runtime > model; proposal ≠ implementation; experiment ≠ result. Seek disconfirmation; prefer narrow support to broad assertion.

Consequential work packet, internal unless trust requires: `source | uncertainty | observations | decision | confidence | action | verification | limits`.
